Physics is a workshop.Let's see how you work.

This is not a physics exam. Nothing to revise, nothing to memorise, and nothing to write. It is nine hands-on stations that show us how you observe, test, measure, build and think — so we can place you in the right group when we open in October 2026.

Works offline. Your report is generated on this device the moment you finish.

Before you begin

  • 1Work alone. A parent or friend helping changes what we see, and that helps nobody.
  • 2No calculator, no phone, no searching. Estimating roughly is a skill we are measuring.
  • 3Some tasks are meant to be hard. We watch how you attack them, not only whether you finish.
  • 4Play with every simulation. Pressing "run" ten times tells us more than answering fast.
  • 5If you truly do not know, pick what you think is most likely and move on. Blank tells us nothing.
  • 6You will never be asked to write about yourself. Everything is tapping, dragging, building and choosing — so your English or Arabic is never what is being judged.
  • 7Finish in one sitting. Closing this page loses your work.
