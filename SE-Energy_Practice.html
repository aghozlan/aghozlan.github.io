What every ME exercise looks like

One storyline, three acts

1

Calculate at A

KE(A), GPE(A), then ME(A). If it is "released from rest", KE(A) = 0, and you must say why.

≈ 2.5 to 3.5 pts · every paper
2

No friction

"Why is ME conserved?" Because friction (air resistance) is neglected. Then ME(A) = ME(B) gives a speed or a height.

≈ 2 to 3 pts · 8 of 10 papers
3

In reality

ME decreases. Find the new ME, the loss, the friction force f, or the form it takes: thermal energy.

≈ 1.5 to 2.5 pts · 6 of 10 papers
Newer twist since 2019

Act 2 or 3 is often asked as "Choose with justification": a), b) or c). Since 2022 the data may also come in a table of speeds, heights or energies.

The course · as in the national textbook, Chapter 1

Everything you need, nothing more

1 · Work

Sweating is not working

A force does work only if its point of application moves and it acts, totally or partly, in the direction of motion. Pushing a wall that does not move: 0 J. The weight and the normal force on a pram rolling along a flat road: 0 J.

Definition

The work of a constant force \(F\) causing a displacement \(d\) parallel to \(F\):

\[W = F \times d \qquad \text{J} = \text{N}\times\text{m}\]

Work of the weight: \(W = m\,g\,h\) for a height change \(h\), whatever the path. The 2019 paper asks exactly this.

Diver, 80 kg, falls 6 m to the water. Work of the weight?J
2 · Energy & GPE

Stored by position

A system has energy if it is able to do work. Lift a stone: your work is stored in the system [stone, Earth] as gravitational potential energy.

Formula
\[GPE = m\,g\,h\]

\(h\) is measured from the reference level, where GPE = 0. Below it, GPE < 0.

Sled + child, 85 kg, at A 1.8 m above the reference. GPE(A)?J
3 · KE

Stored by speed

The kinetic energy of an object is the energy it possesses by virtue of its motion. Speed counts twice: double the speed and the KE is multiplied by 4.

Formula
\[KE = \tfrac12\,m\,v^2 \qquad v = \sqrt{\frac{2\,KE}{m}}\]

Mass in kg (100 g = 0.1 kg), speed in m/s (km/h ÷ 3.6).

Ball, 2 kg, launched at 12 m/s. KE?J
4 · ME

Two pockets, one wallet

The mechanical energy of a system at an instant is the sum of its kinetic and potential energies at that instant.

Definition and principle
\[ME = KE + GPE\]

In the absence of friction and air resistance, the ME of a system remains constant: a gain in one form equals the loss in the other.

From rest at 1.8 m, no friction. Speed at the bottom (reference)?m/s
5 · Friction

ME leaks out as heat

With friction or air resistance, ME is not conserved: it decreases. The lost ME appears as thermal energy (heat).

Two ways the papers write it
\[ME_{B} - ME_{C} = f \times BC \quad (2017)\]
\[\Delta ME = ME_{f} - ME_{i} = -f \times d \quad (2015)\]
ME(B) = 1.5 J, stops at C on the reference, BC = 2 m. f?N
6 · Bridge to Ch. 2–3

Efficiency

Energy exercises on power plants reuse \(mgh\). Water falling through a height \(h\) loses GPE, and the plant turns part of it into electrical energy.

Efficiency
\[r = \frac{\text{useful energy (or power)}}{\text{received energy (or power)}}\]
Markaba plant: useful 14 × 10⁶ J each second, r = 35 %. Received energy each second?J
Say why it is zero

"KE(A) = 0 because it is released from rest." "GPE(C) = 0 since C is on the reference level." "KE(B) = 0 at the maximum height." The marking keys give marks for the reason.

Highest point

v = 0, so KE = 0 and ME = GPE. This gives \(h_{max} = \dfrac{ME}{m\,g}\).

Not in your syllabus

Elastic energy ½ k x² is not required in SE. A drawn bow or a spring is only named as stored potential energy.

Before the exam

Memory card

Work
\(W = F\,d\)

F constant and parallel to d. Weight: \(W = mgh\), any path.

Gravitational PE
\(GPE = m\,g\,h\)

h from the reference level. Zero on it.

Kinetic energy
\(KE = \tfrac12 m v^2\)

m in kg, v in m/s. \(v = \sqrt{2KE/m}\).

Mechanical energy
\(ME = KE + GPE\)

No friction: ME(A) = ME(B).

With friction
\(ME_i - ME_f = f\,d\)

The loss becomes thermal energy.

Efficiency
\(r = \dfrac{\text{useful}}{\text{received}}\)

Energy or power, same formula.

Decode the verbs

CalculateFormula, numbers substituted, result with its unit.
DeduceUse the result you just found, in one line.
DetermineSeveral steps: write ME, cancel what is zero and say why, solve.
Show thatReach the given value. Use it in the next questions even if you could not prove it.
Why? / JustifyOne sentence with the physical reason: "friction is neglected", "same height", "on the reference level".
Specify / IndicateThe value or the name, with a short reason.
Choose with justificationThe letter and the reason. The letter alone earns very little.
