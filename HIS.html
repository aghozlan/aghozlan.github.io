Vision رؤيتنا

A concept-based curriculum, built around how students think.

HIS describes itself as a progressive pre-kindergarten to Grade 12 learning community, serving students from a range of cultural and social backgrounds and embracing diversity of gender, race and national origin.2

Understanding and knowledge developed through analytical, critical, and creative thinking — while crafting a better future.

Student life & clubs الحياة الطلابية

What happens after the bell is part of the curriculum.

Student life at HIS is run through named offices rather than ad-hoc groups — the structure below mirrors the school's own.5

  • Activities Office & HIS Clubs All years
  • Student Council Elected
  • Life Rhythms, the school publication 75+ issues
  • HIS Radio & TV Student media
  • Service as Action Middle Years
  • Personal Project Middle Years
  • Creativity, Activity, Service Diploma
  • Counselling & Health Offices Wellbeing
  • Student Orientation Every intake

Eight standing committees carry the community side of school life — Activities, Social, Learning Resources, Media, IT, Spatial Aesthetics, Safety, and the Parents' Council.7

The campus الحرم المدرسي
