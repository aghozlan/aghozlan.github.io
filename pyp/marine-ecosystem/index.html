Weather & the ocean

Wind, waves & sea breezes

Sea breeze (daytime)

In the day, land heats up faster than water. Warm air over the land rises, and cool air from the sea rushes in to take its place.

🌬️ Wave maker

Most waves are made by wind blowing over the water. The stronger the wind, the bigger the waves!

🍃🌪️
Gentle breeze🧭 Currents are like rivers inside the sea, pushed by the wind.
Weather & the ocean

How does the ocean make a hurricane?

the eye 👁️ calm centre
🌡️
1 · Warm water

The Sun heats tropical sea water to more than about 27 °C.

💨
2 · Warm, wet air rises

Warm, damp air rises fast. More air rushes in underneath to replace it.

☁️
3 · Giant clouds start to spin

The rising air builds huge storm clouds. Earth's spin makes them turn in a circle.

🌀
4 · A hurricane is born!

Winds blow faster than 119 km/h. Over land it loses its warm-water “fuel” and weakens.

Same storm, different names:

🌎 Hurricane · Atlantic🌏 Typhoon · NW Pacific🌍 Cyclone · Indian Ocean
The Moon & the ocean

Tides: the Moon pulls the sea!

🌊 High tide!

The Moon's gravity pulls on the ocean and makes it bulge. As Earth spins, your beach passes through the bulges, so most beaches get two high tides and two low tides about every day.

Speed

🏆 The Bay of Fundy in Canada has the biggest tides on Earth — the water can rise about 16 metres, as tall as a 5-storey building!

Tiny driftersPhytoplankton

Phytoplankton

  • So small you need a microscope to see one.
  • They make about half of the oxygen on Earth!
  • They are the start of almost every ocean food chain.
tap to flip ↻
Seaweed (algae)Giant kelp

Giant kelp

  • Can grow about half a metre in one day!
  • Air-filled bubbles help it float up to the light.
  • Makes underwater forests — home for sea otters & fish.
tap to flip ↻
A real flowering plantSeagrass

Seagrass

  • A true plant with roots, leaves and even flowers — underwater!
  • Favourite food of sea turtles and manatees.
  • Seagrass meadows grow in the Mediterranean Sea too.
tap to flip ↻
Trees in salt waterMangroves

Mangroves

  • Trees that grow in salty water on tropical coasts.
  • Their stilt-like roots are a safe nursery for baby fish.
  • They protect the coast from storms and big waves.
🔬
Photosynthesis: ☀️ sunlight + 💧 water + 💨 carbon dioxide → 🍬 food + 🫧 oxygen

Ocean habitats

Homes in the ocean 🏠

tap ↻
Warm & shallowCoral reefs

Coral reefs

The “rainforests of the sea”. Corals are tiny animals! Reefs cover less than 1% of the sea floor but are home to about a quarter of all sea creatures.

tap ↻
Cool & rockyKelp forests

Kelp forests

Tall underwater forests in cool water. Sea otters, seals, crabs and fish hide and hunt among the swaying kelp.

tap ↻
Where sea meets landRocky shores & tide pools

Tide pools

Pools left behind when the tide goes out. Crabs, sea stars and anemones must survive waves, sun and drying out!

tap ↻
Far from landThe open ocean

Open ocean

The biggest habitat on Earth! Fast swimmers like tuna, dolphins and sharks travel huge distances. Little fish crowd into “bait balls” to stay safe.

tap ↻
Deep & darkHydrothermal vents

Deep-sea vents

Hot springs on the sea floor! There is no sunlight, so tiny microbes make food from chemicals. Giant tube worms live here.

tap ↻
Freezing coldPolar seas

Polar seas

Icy oceans around the North and South Poles. Penguins, seals, polar bears and whales have thick fat (blubber) to stay warm.
