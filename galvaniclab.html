Cell notation:

ΔE° uses the standard potentials of the two couples (1 mol/L, 25 °C). Rule: ΔE° > 0 → spontaneous; ΔE° > 0.3 V → practically complete. Simplifications: concentrations are used instead of activities, E° is taken as independent of temperature, H₂ is supplied at 1 bar. Real Mg and Al plates read less than their theoretical value because a thin oxide layer covers them.

3 · Explain

What is really happening

⚡ Anode = oxidation

The more reactive metal gives up electrons: Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻. In a galvanic cell it is the negative pole, and it loses mass.

🎯 Cathode = reduction

Ions take those electrons: Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu. It is the positive pole; metal deposits on it and its solution fades.

🌉 The salt bridge

Electrons never enter it. K⁺ ions drift to the cathode side and NO₃⁻ to the anode side, so both solutions stay neutral and the loop closes.

📉 Why voltage changes

E = E° − (0.0592/n)·log Q, and under load V = E − I·r. Concentration, temperature and internal resistance all matter.
