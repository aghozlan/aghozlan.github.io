  2. Write the displacement vector M1M7→. Calculate the average velocity of the trolley between M1 and M7.

/ 10The truck and the motorcycle near Byblos Position graph, time equations and meeting point.

On a straight part of the coastal highway near Byblos, the axis (O,i→) points toward Tripoli and O is at the exit of a gas station. The graph gives the position of a heavy truck in the right lane. At t=0, Rami's motorcycle leaves O from rest.

A The truck

  1. Use the shape of the graph to give the nature of the motion of the truck. What is its acceleration?
  2. Calculate the velocity of the truck. Express it in km/h.
  3. Show that the time equation of the truck is xT=5t+40 (SI).
  4. Write the displacement vector of the truck between t=0 and t=20 s. Calculate its average velocity in this interval. What do you notice?

B The motorcycle

The motorcycle starts with a constant acceleration a=2.5 m/s2.

  1. Give x0 and V0 of the motorcycle. Write vM(t) and show that xM=1.25t2 (SI).
  2. Verify that the motorcycle catches the truck at t=8 s. Give the position of this meeting. Compare the two velocities at this instant: who overtakes whom?
  3. At t=8 s, Rami reaches 72 km/h and keeps this velocity. Give the nature of his new motion and show that, for t≥8 s, xM=20(t−8)+80. How far apart are the two vehicles at t=12 s?
