/ 10Maya's toy car on the ramp Reading a dot print, vectors and time equations.

In the school lab, Maya releases a toy car on a straight ramp. A device marks the position of a point M of the car every τ=40 ms. The axis (O,i→) has its origin at M0 and points in the direction of motion. The figure is not to scale: use the abscissas x=OM of the table.

A Nature of the motion

  1. Why is the motion of M rectilinear?
  2. Complete the row d of the table. Show that Δd=dn+1−dn is constant. Deduce the nature of the motion and the value of the acceleration a.

B Vectors and velocities

  1. Write, in terms of i→ and in metres, the position vector OM4→ and the displacement vector M2M6→.
  2. Calculate the average velocity of M between M2 and M6.
  3. Calculate V1, V3, V4, V5 and V7 and write them in the table. Compare V4 with the answer of question 4. Why are they equal?
  4. On the figure, draw V→3 and V→5. Scale: 1 cm ↔ 0.25 m/s.
  5. Calculate a4 and a6. Do they agree with part A? Is the motion accelerated or decelerated? Justify with the sign of a⋅V.

C Time equations (t=0 when M is at M0)

  1. Using V1=aτ+V0, show that the velocity at M0 is V0=0.25 m/s.
  2. Write v(t) and x(t) in SI units. Check x(t) with the position of M5.
  3. After M8, the car reaches the horizontal floor of the lab: the new dots are all 4.2 cm apart. Give the nature of the new motion, its velocity and its acceleration. Check that this velocity agrees with v(t) at M8.

/ 10Danger on the Beirut–Sidon highway Velocity graph, positions and braking.

At t=0, Rami starts from rest at a traffic light O in Khaldeh and drives on a straight part of the highway toward Sidon. The axis (O,i→) points toward Sidon. The graph gives the velocity of his car for the first 40 s.

A Reading the graph

  1. Give the nature of the motion in phase (1) and in phase (2). Justify.
  2. Calculate the acceleration in each phase.
  3. Read the velocity at t=5 s. Express 20 m/s in km/h. The speed limit is 100 km/h: does Rami respect it?

B Where is Rami?

  1. Give x0, V0 and a for phase (1). Show that x=t2 (SI). Find the position at t=10 s.
  2. In phase (2), x=20(t−10)+100. Explain each number, then show that Rami is at xG=700 m at t=40 s.
  3. Write the displacement vector OG→. Calculate the average velocity between 0 and 40 s. Why is it less than 20 m/s?

C The truck

At t=40 s, at G, Rami sees a truck stopped at P, with GP=70 m. He needs a reaction time of 0.5 s (same velocity), then he brakes. With a new time origin at the start of braking, x=−2.5t2+20t+710 (SI).

  1. Find the distance covered during the reaction time. Show that it agrees with the number 710.
  2. By comparison with x=12at2+V0t+x0, give a, V0 and x0. Write v(t).
  3. Hiba says: "a is negative, so the car slows down." Is her reason enough? Give the correct justification.
  4. Find the braking time and the position where the car stops. Does Rami hit the truck?
  5. Complete the graph for the reaction time and the braking.
