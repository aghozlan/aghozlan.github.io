/ 8A skier in FarayaInclined plane, horizontal plane, then a horizontal launch.

A skier (m=60 kg with equipment) starts from rest at A. He slides down the slope AB (inclined at 30°, AB=50 m), then along the horizontal track BC (BC=150 m). On AB and BC, the friction force is constant: f=60 N. At C he leaves the edge of a 5 m step horizontally and lands at D.

A The slope AB

  1. Draw the forces acting on the skier. Apply the 2nd law and show that his acceleration is a1=4 m/s2.
  2. Find his speed at B and the time taken to go from A to B.
  3. Calculate the normal reaction of the slope.

B The horizontal track BC

  1. Find the acceleration a2 on BC. What is the nature of the motion?
  2. Show that the speed at C is 10 m/s.
  3. Find the time taken from B to C. Which horizontal force should push the skier on BC to keep his speed constant? Which law do you use?

C The jump (origin at C, Ox horizontal, Oy upward)

  1. Write the time equations of the skier after C. Deduce the equation of the trajectory.
  2. Find the horizontal distance between C and the landing point D, and the speed at D.
