Newton's Second Law: Motion of a Projectile

Name: Class: Date:
The idea. A projectile is a body launched in the air with an initial velocity V→0. Air resistance is neglected, so the only external force is its weight. 2nd law: mg→=ma→⇒a→=g→: vertical, downward, the same for all masses. No horizontal force acts, so (1st law) the horizontal velocity stays constant.

Trajectory (eliminate t): y=−g2V02cos2⁡αx2+xtan⁡α: a parabola in the vertical plane that contains V→0.

  • Range (back to the level of O): OP=V02sin⁡2αg, flight time 2tS. Maximum for 45°; α and 90°−α give the same range.
  • Velocity at a point: V=Vx2+Vy2; angle with the horizontal: tan⁡β=|Vy|Vx.
  • Horizontal launch (α=0) from a height h: x=V0t, y=−12gt2, y=−g2V02x2; it lands when y=−h.
Method: ① system, Galilean frame, axes (O, x, y) → ② forces: weight only → ③ 2nd law: a→=g→ → ④ initial conditions → ⑤ Vx,Vy,x,y as functions of t → ⑥ trajectory, summit, landing point, velocity.

Applications

1

A ball rolls off a wallHorizontal launch.

A ball leaves the top O of a wall of height h=1.8 m with a horizontal velocity V0=8 m/s. Air resistance is neglected.

  1. Name the force acting on the ball during its flight. Apply the 2nd law and give the nature of the motion on x and on y.
  2. Write Vx, Vy, x and y as functions of time. Deduce the equation of the trajectory.
  3. Calculate the time to reach the ground and the distance from the foot of the wall to the landing point P.
  4. Find the velocity at P (value and angle with the horizontal). Draw V→x, V→y and V→ at P.
  5. A heavier ball leaves O with the same velocity. Does it land at the same point? Justify.
2

A firefighting plane over the ChoufInitial velocity given by a moving carrier.

A plane flies horizontally at h=80 m above the ground, at a constant velocity of 60 m/s. It drops water at O. Neglect air resistance on the water.

  1. What is the initial velocity of the water just after it leaves the plane? Which law explains it?
  2. Write the time equations of the water. How long does the water take to reach the ground?
  3. At what horizontal distance before the fire F must the pilot drop the water?
  4. Where is the plane when the water reaches the ground? Explain with the 1st law.

A free kick in BeirutOblique launch: summit, obstacle and target.

Ali kicks the ball from O on the ground with V0=20 m/s at 30° above the horizontal. A wall of players (1.8 m high) stands 9.15 m away and the goal (crossbar at 2.44 m) is 30 m away. Neglect air resistance.

  1. Calculate V0x and V0y. Write the time equations and show that the trajectory is y=−x260+0.577x (SI).

  3. Does the ball pass above the wall?
  4. The goalkeeper misses the ball. Is it a goal? Justify.
  5. Calculate the speed of the ball when it crosses the goal line.
