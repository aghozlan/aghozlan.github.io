A particle \( M \) moves in a 2D Cartesian coordinate system \( (O; \vec{i}, \vec{j}) \). At time \( t = 0 \text{ s} \), the particle starts from the origin \( \vec{r}_0 = \vec{0} \) with an initial velocity \( \vec{v}_0 = 2 \vec{i} + 4 \vec{j} \text{ (m/s)} \).
The particle is subjected to a constant acceleration vector \( \vec{a} = -2 \vec{j} \text{ (m/s}^2\text{)} \).
Figure 1: Diagram of the particle's parabolic trajectory.
1. Determine the parametric equations of motion \( x(t) \) and \( y(t) \).
2. Deduce the position vector \( \vec{r}(t) \) of the particle at any instant \( t \).
3. Calculate the magnitude of the displacement vector \( ||\Delta\vec{r}|| \) between \( t_1 = 1 \text{ s} \) and \( t_2 = 3 \text{ s} \).
4. Find the equation of the trajectory \( y(x) \) and specify the geometric nature of the path.
5. At what instant(s) \( t \) does the particle pass through the x-axis?
6. Determine the instant(s) when the particle reaches a vertical position (ordinate) of \( y = 3 \text{ m} \).
