📏 Graticule ruler

Real scopes have no ruler. You count divisions on the grid. When your eyes need help, switch this on and tap two points on the screen.

🧭 Scope etiquette

• Find the trace first: GND coupling → centre it with POS.

• VAR knobs fully clockwise (CAL) or your readings are wrong.

• A bright still spot burns the phosphor. Keep intensity low.

• The ground clip goes on the circuit's earth point only.

3 · Explain

The lab notebook

Readings turn into laws. The notebook copies the instrument settings for you. You only type what your eyes read on the grid.

📐 The five readings

U = y × Sv y = vertical deflection (div), Sv = V/div
T = x × Sh · f = 1 / T x = divisions per period
Ueff = Um / √2 (sinusoidal only)
|φ| = 2π · d / T d = time shift between two traces
τ = RC · τ = L/(R+r) · T₀ = 2π√(LC) · f₀ = 1 / (2π√(LC))

Simplifications on this bench: ideal generator (no internal resistance), 0.7 V diode drop, ideal capacitor, and coil resistance r included where shown.
