    Simplified model: 1 mol/L solutions, 25 °C, large plates, a wide salt bridge and a small bulb, so the meter stays close to the standard cell voltage. Real magnesium plates read less, because a thin oxide layer covers them. Solution colours follow the real ions: Cu²⁺ blue, Fe²⁺ pale green, the others colourless.
